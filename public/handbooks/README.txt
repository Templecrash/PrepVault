PrepVault emergency handbook collection
Downloaded and source-checked: 2026-10-26

These are complete, unmodified copies of nine publisher-authored PDFs. sources.json
contains publisher, edition, source/download URLs, citation, rights evidence, page
count, byte count and SHA-256 for every file. Labels and descriptions are PrepVault
catalog metadata, not additions to the documents or medical advice.

The collection is provided for personal, noncommercial preparedness reference.
WHO/ICRC Basic Emergency Care is CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 IGO.
Stanford Emergency Manual is CC BY-NC-ND 4.0.
These two publications must not be bundled into commercial use without the
necessary additional permission. The Stanford PDF must not be redistributed in
modified form. The U.S. government publications have separate public-domain terms;
the entire collection must not be labelled public domain.

Material was developed by the credited agencies and authors. Their inclusion,
including links to CDC, HHS and other government websites, does not imply that
CDC, HHS, the U.S. Government, WHO, ICRC, Stanford or any other publisher endorses
PrepVault or its services. Original publications are available free from publisher
websites; the WHO copy was acquired from Medbox's humanitarian-library mirror.
Author/institution logos appear only inside the original publisher documents.

Medical and surgical titles are intended for trained healthcare professionals.
CERT material complements practical training. Publication dates are retained:
these are reference editions, not a claim that every protocol remains current.
In particular, WHO's 2018 workbook predates its November 2025 postpartum
haemorrhage update. Check current local clinical protocols and publisher updates.
The Stanford webpage calls its main revision 2021; its V4.4 PDF cover says 2022.
The CDC generator PDF is undated; its catalog edition identifies the current
publisher page's review date, not a new PDF publication date.

The collection deliberately does not rehost Borden Institute Emergency War
Surgery: its front matter reserves third-party copyrights and does not grant
blanket permission to redistribute the complete book. Its official source is
https://medcoe.army.mil/borden-tb-ews/

Offline notes: the downloaded PDFs remain readable without internet access in a
PDF reader. External links inside the documents require internet access. Keep a
separate copy outside browser storage for dependable access. Browser storage may
be cleared by the browser or device; saving inside an app is not a permanent backup.
