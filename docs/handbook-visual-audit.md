# Bundled handbook visual audit

Audit date: 2026-10-28. Scope: the nine original PDFs listed in `public/handbooks/sources.json`, totalling 659 pages. This audits picture coverage and reader navigation; it is not a clinical review or confirmation that every historical recommendation remains current.

All 659 pages were text-extracted and rendered into labelled contact sheets. Every contact sheet was visually inspected to distinguish task figures, diagrams and supporting equipment pictures from covers, logos, decorative icons and text-only tables. Twenty-two selected PDF pages were additionally rendered at 1,200-pixel height/width limit and visually inspected for the descriptions below. Page numbers in the metadata are **one-based PDF positions**, which can differ from the printed page labels.

The originals were not changed, cropped, annotated or republished as extracted figures. Attribution, licensing, professional-audience notices and edition caveats remain in the existing catalogue and PDFs. The new metadata points the reader to intact pages. QA renders are local artifacts under `../../../outputs/handbook-visual-audit/` relative to this document, not distributed manual assets.

## Skill shortcuts

`src/handbook-visuals.js` explicitly covers all 16 existing skill IDs. Five skills receive suitable handbook-picture links: minor wound care, drinking water, food outages, evacuation and generator/CO safety. Water has two links; the other four have one each. The remaining 11 have empty arrays because these nine PDFs do not supply an appropriate picture for those tasks. This is deliberate coverage metadata, not a claim that the tasks are inherently text-only.

The skill mapping contains only catalogue entries whose audience is `everyone`. It never links the Stanford or WHO clinical procedure figures into beginner Skills. A handbook's `illustrated` status means useful original visual material was identified, not that every task or every page has an explanatory picture. Short factsheets with supporting pictograms say so in their summaries.

## Verification and limits

- All nine files' SHA-256 digests were checked against the existing source catalogue after the audit; originals match.
- Every metadata page is an integer within that PDF's declared page count. Every skill shortcut resolves to a listed visual page in a public-audience handbook.
- All 659 page thumbnails were inspected; only the 22 pages listed above were inspected individually at higher resolution. This is an exhaustive page-presence scan with a targeted detailed visual review, not a line-by-line technical or clinical certification.
- No third-party illustrations were downloaded or repackaged, no professional diagrams were redrawn, and no original manual licensing or hashes were changed.
